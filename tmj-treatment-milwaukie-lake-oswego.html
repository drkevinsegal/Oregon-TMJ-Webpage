What Makes This Area Underserved for TMJ Care

The Portland metro has no shortage of providers who claim to treat TMJ. But when you look closely, most fall into one of a few categories:

  • General dentists who offer a night guard as the primary treatment — useful for protecting teeth but often doesn't address the underlying joint or muscle problem
  • Oral surgeons and dental specialists based in Northwest Portland or the west side, focused on appliances, injections, or surgery
  • Physical therapy clinics that treat TMJ alongside dozens of other conditions, without a specialty focus
  • General chiropractors who list TMJ as one of many conditions they see, but aren't specifically trained in jaw treatment

What's largely missing in the Milwaukie, Lake Oswego, and Oregon City corridor is a dedicated, chiropractic-focused TMJ specialty practice — one that looks at the jaw and the neck together, without pushing you toward dental appliances or surgery as a first step.

Oregon TMJ is built specifically around temporomandibular disorder. It's not a side service — it's the whole point. That focus means more targeted assessment, more specific treatment, and more attention to the factors that are actually driving your symptoms.

Is Chiropractic TMJ Care Right for You?

Chiropractic care for TMJ tends to work best for patients who:

  • Have jaw pain, clicking, or limited movement that hasn't responded to a night guard or dental treatment
  • Also have neck stiffness, headaches, or shoulder tension alongside their jaw symptoms
  • Want a non-surgical, non-injection approach as a first or next step
  • Have jaw symptoms that followed a car accident, whiplash, or other physical trauma
  • Are looking for someone to explain what's actually going on — not just hand them an appliance

A Note on Insurance and Cost

Many patients are surprised to find that chiropractic care is often more affordable than dental TMJ treatment. Night guards can cost hundreds to thousands of dollars and often need to be replaced. Botox injections are typically not covered by insurance and require regular repeats. Surgical options involve significant cost and recovery time.

Chiropractic care for TMJ is covered by some insurance plans — though coverage specifically for TMJ-related treatment varies. If your symptoms began after a car accident, chiropractic TMJ care is often covered under your PIP (personal injury protection) auto insurance with no out-of-pocket cost for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Oregon TMJ only for people with jaw pain?
No — many patients come to us for symptoms they don't initially connect to their jaw, including chronic headaches, ear pressure or tinnitus, dizziness, and neck pain. If you're not sure whether your symptoms are TMJ-related, a proper evaluation will tell you.

How far do patients travel to see Dr. Segal?
Most patients come from within the Portland metro — Milwaukie, Lake Oswego, Oregon City, Clackamas, Happy Valley, West Linn, and Southeast Portland. We also see patients from further afield who haven't found a chiropractic-focused TMJ provider closer to home. The office at 6501 SE King Road is easily accessible from Highway 224 and I-205.

Does Oregon TMJ accept insurance?
Yes — Oregon TMJ is in-network with many major plans including Regence, PacificSource, Providence, Moda, Kaiser, United Healthcare, Cigna, Medicare, and VA. PIP coverage for auto accident patients is also accepted. See our full TMJ insurance guide for details on what's typically covered and what to verify before your visit.

What's the difference between seeing a chiropractor for TMJ versus a dentist?
Dental TMJ care focuses on the teeth, bite, and jaw joint in isolation — typically resulting in night guards, splints, or occlusal adjustments. Chiropractic TMJ care addresses the jaw, cervical spine, posture, and the surrounding muscles as a connected system. For patients who haven't responded to dental approaches, this whole-body view is often the missing piece. See our article on TMJ treatment that hasn't worked for more on this distinction.
