About Oregon TMJ

A Conservative, Non-Surgical Approach to TMJ Care

Oregon TMJ is a chiropractic-focused clinic specializing exclusively in temporomandibular joint disorder (TMJD). Led by Dr. Kevin Segal, DC, we provide a whole-body, biomechanical approach to jaw pain — treating the muscles, joints, cervical spine, and posture that drive TMJ symptoms.

We are an appropriate referral destination for patients who have not responded to dental appliances or bite adjustment, patients seeking a non-surgical alternative before considering joint surgery, and patients whose jaw symptoms appear connected to cervical spine dysfunction, forward head posture, or chronic muscle tension.

Jaw pain & facial tension
TMJ clicking & locking
TMJ-related headaches
Bruxism & clenching
Ear pain & fullness
Neck pain & posture issues
Whiplash-related jaw pain
Forward head posture

How to Refer

Simple Referral Process

1

Download & Complete the Form

Download the referral form above. Complete provider and patient sections. Insurance information is helpful but not required.

2

Fax to (503) 788-8020

Fax the completed form directly to our office. Alternatively, give the form to your patient to bring to their first appointment.

3

Patient Schedules Their Appointment

Your patient can book online via our website or call (503) 774-8525. We will follow up with a report of findings upon request.
